## [kind: PROC_STEP] [lang: pyspark] Procedures and summaries

Use `groupBy().agg()` with named `pyspark.sql.functions` aggregates for PROC
MEANS, SUMMARY, and FREQ-style summaries. Use `join` with an explicit join
condition and join type for SQL and merge logic. For statistical procedures or
reporting features without an equivalent DataFrame operation, retain the SAS
intent in the risks and identify the required Databricks library or review.
